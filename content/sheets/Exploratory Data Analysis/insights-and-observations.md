# Insights and Observations

Close EDA by turning observations into decisions, risks, and questions for the next stage.

## Record

- Important patterns, segments, and trends.
- Data quality issues and their likely causes.
- Anomalies that need investigation.
- Variables that may be useful, redundant, or misleading.
- Assumptions that affect analysis or model choice.

## Validate

Separate evidence from interpretation. Note the population, time window, sample size, and filters behind each finding. Identify follow-up checks before making a business or modeling decision.

## Handoff checklist

- Schema and data grain are documented.
- Missing values and outlier treatments are justified.
- Distributions and relationships have been reviewed.
- Potential leakage and confounding have been considered.
- Open questions and next actions have owners.
